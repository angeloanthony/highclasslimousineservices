Dinosaur National Monument is twenty miles east of Vernal — a 25-minute drive on US-40 past Jensen, into one of the most paleontologically significant landscapes on the continent. The Quarry Exhibit Hall, the Tour of the Tilted Rocks, the Cub Creek petroglyph panels, the Green River winding through 800-foot canyon walls. Most visitors see all of it through a windshield, in a rental car, fighting the GPS. Our visitors see it through tinted glass with a chauffeur who has been there fifty times.

We run limousine tours and shuttle service directly to and from Dinosaur National Monument from Vernal hotels, the airport, or wherever your day starts. Call [**(435) 219-9447**](tel:+14352199447). We'll plan the route around what you actually want to see.
</StoryBlock>

<FeatureRow image="/images/tour-1.webp" alt="Limousine at Dinosaur National Monument visitor center" eyebrow="The Route" title="From Vernal," titleScript="east on US-40.">
The standard Dinosaur trip runs east out of Vernal on US-40, past Naples, through Jensen, and into the Quarry access road. About 20 miles, 25 minutes, all paved. We can pick up at any Vernal hotel, the visitor center, or the airport. The limousine handles the paved park roads inside the monument cleanly — Quarry Visitor Center, the Tour of the Tilted Rocks scenic drive, the Cub Creek pullouts.

For Echo Park, Yampa Bench, or any of the unpaved interior routes, we'll suggest a different vehicle for that leg — the stretch limo isn't built for those roads, and we'd rather tell you that up front than break an axle three miles in.
</FeatureRow>

<FeatureRow image="/images/tour-3.webp" alt="Sunset at Dinosaur National Monument scenic overlook" eyebrow="The Day" title="What we usually see" titleScript="on a tour." reverse>
Most half-day tours hit the highlights: Quarry Exhibit Hall (the main wall of in-situ dinosaur fossils), Tour of the Tilted Rocks scenic drive with stops at Split Mountain and Cub Creek petroglyphs, and a Green River overlook. Full-day extends to the McKee Springs petroglyph panel, Josie Bassett Morris Cabin, and time at Echo Park Overlook.

You can ride between every stop in the cabin or get out at each one — we'll wait. No rushing, no lot-circling, no logistics burning your day.
</FeatureRow>

### How far is Dinosaur National Monument from Vernal?

About 20 miles east via US-40 — a 25-minute drive past Jensen.

### Can the limo go into the park?

Yes on paved roads (Quarry Visitor Center, Tour of the Tilted Rocks). No on unpaved interior roads (Echo Park, Yampa Bench) — we'll suggest a different vehicle for those.

### How much does it cost?

Half-day: $400–$700. Full-day: $700–$1,200. Airport-direct: $1,000–$1,800. Park entrance fee ($25) is separate.

### Airport pickups straight to Dinosaur?

Yes — SLC International, Vernal Regional, or Grand Junction. We pick up, drive direct to the monument, tour, then drop you at your hotel.

### Best time of year?

April through October for full access. Winter trips are doable in mild weather but some scenic roads may be closed seasonally — call to confirm.
