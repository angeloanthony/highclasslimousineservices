### How long is the drive from Vernal to SLC airport?

About 175 miles, 3–3.5 hours under normal conditions via US-40 and I-80. Winter weather on Daniels Summit or summer construction in Parley's can add 30–60 minutes; we plan for it.

### How much is a Vernal to SLC limo?

From $350 one-way (flat rate). Round trips with wait: $650–$800. Hourly available at $135–$200/hr.

### Do you track flights?

Yes — every flight, real-time. Adjustments for delays are automatic and not billed.

### What time should I be picked up for a morning flight?

Four hours before scheduled takeoff. That's drive time + check-in + security + buffer. For 6 a.m. flights, expect a 2 a.m. pickup. We're already awake.
