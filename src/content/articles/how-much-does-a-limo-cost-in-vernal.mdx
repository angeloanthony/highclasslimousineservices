<SplitRow eyebrow="What Changes The Number" title="Six things that" titleScript="move the price.">
<Fragment slot="left">
The hourly rate is the base. From there, six factors push the final invoice up or down.

- **Day of the week:** Saturdays are 10–20% higher than weekday bookings.
- **Season:** Prom weekend and peak wedding season (May–September) are higher than off-season.
- **Vehicle size:** A 14-passenger stretch is roughly 25% more per hour than an 8-passenger.
- **Total hours:** Longer bookings get better per-hour rates. An 8-hour wedding day is cheaper per hour than a 3-hour minimum.
- **Distance:** Long-haul transfers (SLC, Flaming Gorge, Dinosaur Monument) are flat-rate. In-town hourly stays at standard rates.
- **Gratuity:** 18% is industry standard and built into our quotes. Some companies quote without it — always ask.
</Fragment>
<Fragment slot="right">
### What's Always Included

Chauffeur (tuxedoed for formal events, business-attire for executive transfers), fuel, bottled water, ice, sound system with phone hookup, LED interior lighting, and a clean exterior. We don't tack on fuel surcharges or "vehicle prep" fees after the fact.

### What Costs Extra

Champagne service ($45 per bottle), red carpet rollout (free for weddings, $25 for other events), additional stops beyond the original itinerary (billed at the hourly rate), and any toll roads (rare in our service area).
