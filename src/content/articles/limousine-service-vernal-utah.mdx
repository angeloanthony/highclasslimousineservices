<StoryBlock>
### Booking too late.

Wedding season runs April through October in the Basin. Prom is one weekend in April for Uintah High and similar weekends for surrounding schools. If you wait until 30 days out, the date may already be gone — to a wedding, a graduation, or a corporate run we committed to weeks earlier. Book as far ahead as you can. For weddings and prom: 4–8 weeks minimum. For airport runs: 48–72 hours is usually fine.

### Choosing on price alone.

If a quote comes in dramatically below market, ask why. Common reasons: outdated vehicle, no commercial insurance, drivers without proper licensing, or surprise fees layered on at the end. The cost of a wedding ride that breaks down on the way to the ceremony is not measurable in dollars.

### Not asking about the route.

"It's only ten miles" can mean ten miles of construction, a closed canyon road, or a venue with no turnaround for a 30-foot stretch. Tell your chauffeur the full route in advance. We'll either confirm it works or suggest the alternative that actually does.

### Forgetting about the after-party.

Most prom and party bookings end at the dance — and then six teenagers are stuck calling Ubers at midnight. Build the after-party leg into the original booking. It's almost always cheaper than reactive ride-shares and infinitely safer.
</StoryBlock>

### How much does limousine service in Vernal, Utah cost?

Stretch limousine service in Vernal typically runs $135–$200 per hour with a 3-hour minimum. Airport transfers to Salt Lake City International start at $350 one-way (flat rate). Wedding packages range $500–$1,500 depending on hours and add-ons. Gratuity (15–20%) and tax are added at booking. [Full pricing breakdown here.](/how-much-does-a-limo-cost-in-vernal)

### What's your service area?

We're based in Vernal and serve the entire Uintah Basin — Roosevelt, Duchesne, Jensen, Naples, Maeser — plus regional destinations like [Dinosaur National Monument](/limo-near-dinosaur-national-monument), [Flaming Gorge](/limo-service-flaming-gorge), Grand Junction Regional Airport, and Salt Lake City International.

### How many people fit in the limo?

Up to 10 passengers comfortably, with full leather seating, mood lighting, and a premium sound system. Larger groups can be split across two trips or paired with a follow-vehicle — call us to plan it.

### How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, prom, and weekend events: 4–8 weeks minimum. For airport transfers and weeknight bookings: 48–72 hours is usually enough. The earlier you book, the more flexibility you have on routing and timing. [Request a quote here.](/contact#quote)

### Are you locally owned, or part of a national chain?

We're locally owned, family-run, and operate from Vernal. We're not a franchise. When you call [(435) 219-9447](tel:+14352199447), you're calling neighbors who live in the Basin, drive these roads daily, and answer for the entire experience personally.

### Do you provide drinks or food in the limo?

Chilled water and champagne flutes are stocked in the bar at no extra charge. Anything beyond that — alcohol, snacks, decorations — is welcome but BYO, and subject to Utah law (no alcohol on bookings involving minors, including prom and school events).

### What happens if my flight is delayed?

For airport pickups we track your flight in real time and adjust the pickup automatically. You won't pay for our wait time on a delay that's outside your control. [Full airport service details.](/airport)

### Is there a cancellation policy?

Yes — cancellations more than 72 hours before the booking refund the deposit in full. Inside 72 hours, the deposit may be retained. Weather cancellations are handled case by case. Full [terms here](/terms).
