Round trips from Vernal **start at $700**: we drive you there, wait during your appointment, and bring you home. Call [**(435) 219-9447**](tel:+14352199447).

<Section>
<StoryBlock dropCap>
Specialist care for the Basin usually means a three-hour drive each way. Vernal to Salt Lake City is about 175 miles on US-40 and I-80. That's six hours of driving in one day, often after an early start, sometimes after a procedure that leaves you in no shape to get behind the wheel.

So hand it to us. You ride in the back of a black stretch limousine, with room to stretch out and a family member beside you. We know the route, we leave with time to spare, and we're waiting when you walk out.
</StoryBlock>

<FeatureRow image="/images/4.webp" alt="Black stretch limousine on the road at golden hour" eyebrow="The Trip" title="There, waiting," titleScript="and home.">
We pick you up at your door in Vernal, drive you to your appointment, and wait while you're seen. When you're done, the car is ready and we bring you home the same day.

Common destinations include the University of Utah Hospital and Huntsman Cancer Institute in Salt Lake City, Intermountain Medical Center in Murray, Utah Valley Hospital in Provo, and St. Mary's Medical Center in Grand Junction. If your appointment is somewhere else in those areas, we'll take you there too.
</FeatureRow>

<FeatureRow image="/images/hero-poster.webp" alt="Stretch limousine cabin with wide leather seating" eyebrow="The Ride" title="Three hours," titleScript="in comfort." reverse>
Wide leather seats, climate control, bottled water, and a phone charger. Rest on the way there, sleep on the way home.

There's room for a spouse, a parent or a caregiver. Our stretch limousines seat 8–10 or 12–14 passengers.
</FeatureRow>

<FeatureRow image="/images/gallery-11.webp" alt="Stretch limousine interior with leather seating and soft lighting" eyebrow="Before You Book" title="What we can" titleScript="and can't do.">
**Wheelchairs:** a folding wheelchair rides along with you. You need to be able to move from the wheelchair into a seat, because our limousines have no ramp or lift.

**Not medical transport:** this is private chauffeured transportation. Our chauffeurs are not medical staff and the vehicles carry no medical equipment. For a medical emergency, call 911.
</FeatureRow>
