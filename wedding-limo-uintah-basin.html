Uintah Basin Weddings in a Limousine.

From the church doors to the reception barn. A chauffeur who knows every back road in the Basin.

High Class Limousine Services provides wedding transportation across the entire Uintah Basin — Vernal, Roosevelt, Duchesne, Jensen, Naples, Maeser, and the rural ranches and event venues between them. Stretch limousine packages run $500–$1,500 depending on hours and route. We coordinate ceremony departures, bridal party transport, parent transport, and grand-getaway exits. Common Basin venues include the LDS temple sites, McKee Springs, Pelican Lake-area barns, and private family ranches. Book 6–9 months ahead for peak season.

A Basin wedding is its own thing. The ceremony might be at a church in Vernal, the reception at a family ranch outside Roosevelt, and the photos somewhere up Dry Fork Canyon in between. The driving matters more than people think.

We've run limos for weddings in every corner of the Uintah Basin — from intimate elopements at the McKee Springs petroglyphs to 200-guest receptions at family-built barns out past Tridell. Every wedding has a route nobody talks about, and getting the bride and the bridal party from one point to another on time, comfortably, in a vehicle that doubles as a backdrop for fifty photos, is something we treat as a real responsibility.

Where We Serve

The whole Basin.

Vernal & Naples

Ceremonies at the Vernal Utah Temple, receptions at the Western Park Convention Center, the Bingham Center, or private homes. The shortest routes — usually 3–4 hours of limo time covers everything.

Roosevelt & Surrounding

Many Roosevelt weddings happen at the Roosevelt LDS Stake Center or family ranches between Roosevelt and Myton. We typically run 4–6 hours, accounting for the longer drive time and any photo stops along Highway 40.

Duchesne & Tabiona

Smaller weddings often, but the routes can stretch out — especially when the reception is up at Strawberry or out toward Tabiona. 5–7 hour packages are common here.

Jensen, Tridell, Altamont

Rural ceremonies and barn receptions. We're comfortable on dirt roads up to a point — anything beyond a graded ranch road, we'll discuss in advance.

Pricing

Wedding packages.

Wedding limo costs in the Basin depend on three things: how many hours, how far the route runs, and how many vehicles you need. Most weddings book a single stretch limo for the bride and bridal party, with the option of adding a second limo for parents or grandparents.

  • 4-hour ceremony & reception package: $500–$800
  • 6-hour full-day package (Vernal area): $750–$1,200
  • 8-hour all-day package (Basin-wide travel): $1,000–$1,500
  • Grand getaway only (1.5–2 hours): $250–$400
  • Second limo (parents' transport): Discounted 15% when booked together

We require a deposit at booking and the balance one week before the wedding date. No surprise charges at the end of the night — the package quoted is the package billed.

What's Included

Champagne gold ribbon on the hood, polished exterior, tuxedoed chauffeur, complimentary champagne or sparkling cider, bottled water, ice, and red carpet rollout at the ceremony. We arrive 30 minutes early at every pickup, every time. The chauffeur stays in his vehicle during the ceremony — no wandering off, no being elsewhere when the bride steps out.

Decoration

We handle the basics — ribbons, just-married signage if requested, and aisle red carpet. Anything more elaborate (floral arrangements on the limo, custom vinyl) needs to be coordinated with your florist or decorator and we'll work with them directly.

A Sample Day

What a Basin wedding day looks like.

11:00 AM — Limo arrives at the bride's home in Vernal. Final photos with parents.

11:30 AM — Depart for the Vernal Utah Temple. Bridesmaids and bride in the limo.

12:30 PM — Ceremony concludes. Limo holds curbside.

1:00 PM — Wedding party photos, sometimes 30–45 minutes around the temple grounds. Limo waits.

2:00 PM — Drive to additional photo location — Dry Fork Canyon, Steinaker Reservoir, or Red Fleet are common.

3:30 PM — Return to the reception venue. Bride and groom in the limo arrive last for the grand entrance.

5:00 PM — Reception in full swing. Limo released or held for the grand getaway, depending on the package.

9:00 PM — Grand getaway. Bride and groom climb in. Sparklers in the parking lot. The limo pulls away. The day is done.

Common Questions

Wedding questions.

How far in advance should we book?

Six to nine months for peak wedding season (May–October) in the Basin. We book one wedding per Saturday in peak months, so calendar slots are limited.

What happens if it rains or snows?

The limo runs in any weather. We carry umbrellas for the chauffeur to escort the bride between the limo and the venue. We've worked Basin weddings in driving rain and in late-October flurries — the show goes on.

Can the limo handle the dirt road to our family ranch?

A graded dirt road, generally yes. A two-track or anything washboarded, no — and we'll tell you that before booking. If the access road is rough, we can drop at the highway and arrange shuttles for the last mile.

Do we need to provide anything?

No. Champagne, ice, water, glasses, ribbons — all handled. If you want specific decorations, let us know in advance.

Is gratuity included?

Eighteen percent gratuity is included in your quoted package price. Additional tipping is at your discretion and is never expected.
