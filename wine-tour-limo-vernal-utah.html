Wine Tour Limousine in Eastern Utah

Curated routes from Vernal to Park City. Glassware stocked. You ride, we drive.

High Class Limousine Services runs curated wine tour limousine packages from Vernal across the Uintah Basin and out to Park City and Heber Valley wine country. 3 to 5 stops over 4–6 hours typical, glassware and chilled water provided, multi-stop routing built around your reservations. $135–$200/hr (3-hour minimum); typical Vernal tours $540–$1,200; Park City extended trips $800–$1,500. Call (435) 219-9447.

A wine tour limousine in eastern Utah doesn't look quite like Napa — but for the small, curated, beautifully-routed afternoon trip, the Uintah Basin and the drive west into Heber Valley actually deliver something unique. Craft cocktail bars in Park City. Wine-forward dinner spots in Heber. The Vernal Brewing Company. Boutique tasting rooms tucked into mountain towns nobody outside Utah has heard of. Strung together on a thoughtful itinerary, with someone else doing the driving, the day adds up to something memorable.

We design wine tour limousine packages around your group, your reservations, and your comfort level with the drive. The vehicle stays with you between stops. You sample what you want at each one. Nobody worries about who's driving home. Call (435) 219-9447 to plan the day.

The Itinerary

Three to five stops. Carefully routed.

Most wine tour bookings cover 3–5 destinations across 4–6 hours. A typical Vernal-area afternoon: lunch with a wine pairing in town, a stop at the Vernal Brewing Co., a tasting at a craft cocktail bar, dinner reservation. The full Park City extended trip runs longer: drive west, lunch in Heber, two tasting rooms, dinner on Main Street, return drive after.

You don't need to know every detail in advance. Tell us your group size, the budget, the kind of day you want, and we'll suggest the route. Confirm reservations through us if you'd like — we know which Park City restaurants need eight weeks of lead time and which take walk-ins.

In the Cabin

Glassware ready. Climate set.

Chilled water and champagne flutes are stocked at no charge. Bring your samples or your own bottles between stops — we'll keep them cool. Cabin is climate-controlled both directions; lighting is dimmable; sound system is yours.

Group sizes up to 10. For couples or small groups, the cabin feels almost spa-quiet between stops. For larger parties — anniversaries, milestone birthdays, ladies' weekend — it turns into the celebration on wheels.

Pricing

Per-hour, all-in.

Wine tour limo bookings run our standard $135–$200 per hour with a 3-hour minimum. A typical Vernal-area afternoon (4–6 hours) lands at $540–$1,200. The full Park City / Heber extended tour runs $800–$1,500 depending on hours and mileage.

Reservation fees, tasting fees, and meal costs at each stop are paid directly by you to the venue — we just handle transportation. Gratuity (15–20%) and tax are added at booking.

FAQ

Wine tour questions.

Are there actual wineries in Vernal?

Vernal doesn't have on-site wineries; the closest dedicated wine country is Park City and the Heber Valley, about 3 hours west. Local Vernal stops include the Vernal Brewing Co. and select wine-forward restaurants and bars.

How does the limo wine tour work?

You pick the stops (or we suggest them). The limo stays with you the entire afternoon — between every stop, into the cabin, out at the next one. No driving, no parking, no Uber roulette.

How much does it cost?

$135–$200/hr (3-hour minimum). Most tours: $540–$1,200. Park City extended: $800–$1,500.

Can we drink between stops?

Yes — Utah law permits it for guests 21+. We provide glassware; you bring the wine.

How early should we book?

Vernal-only afternoon tours: 2–3 weeks. Park City extended trips, especially during Sundance or summer season: 6–8 weeks.
