Bachelor Party Limo in Vernal, Utah

Ten seats. One driver. Whatever the night turns into.

High Class Limousine Services runs bachelor party limos out of Vernal across the Uintah Basin and to Salt Lake City. Up to 10 passengers, multi-stop routing built around your itinerary, $135–$200 per hour with a 3-hour minimum (most bachelor nights run $540–$1,200; SLC weekends $800–$1,500). Locally owned, fully insured, available 7 days a week. Call (435) 219-9447.

A bachelor party limo is the difference between a night you talk about and a night somebody has to apologize for the next morning. Nobody driving. Nobody pulled over on US-40 at 1 a.m. Nobody arguing about whose turn it is to be the designated whatever. The limo handles it. You handle the rest.

We run bachelor party bookings out of Vernal across the entire Uintah Basin and on to Salt Lake City for the bigger weekends. Up to 10 passengers, full multi-stop routing, a sound system loud enough to be the start of the night, and a chauffeur who has seen everything and judges nothing. Call (435) 219-9447 to set the date.

The Itinerary

Whatever the night turns into.

Most Vernal bachelor parties book a 4–6 hour package: pickup, dinner (steakhouse, brewery, or whatever the groom actually likes), drinks, somewhere with a pool table or a dance floor, and a clean ride home. The longer-haul version is a Saturday SLC run — drive down with brunch stops, hotel check-in, then a UFC card or a Jazz game and a long night out. We've routed every variation.

You don't need to know the whole itinerary in advance. Tell us the start, the dinner reservation, and roughly what you want next. We'll figure out the rest as the night moves.

The Cabin

Built for a crew of ten.

Leather seating wraps the cabin. Mood lighting glows along the ceiling. The bar is stocked with chilled water and champagne flutes — the rest is BYO and 21+. Bluetooth your music straight in. The system is tuned for the cabin: it actually sounds good, not the rattly mess you get in most limos.

  • Premium sound system
  • Tinted windows for privacy
  • Multi-stop routing included
  • Professional, discreet chauffeur
Pricing

What it actually costs.

Vernal bachelor party bookings run $135–$200 per hour with a 3-hour minimum. A standard 4–6 hour Vernal-area bachelor night lands at $540–$1,200. The full SLC weekend package runs $800–$1,500 depending on hours and mileage.

Gratuity (15–20%) and tax are added at booking. No per-mile fees, no hidden surcharges. For full pricing across services, see how much a limo costs in Vernal.

FAQ

Bachelor party questions.

How much does a bachelor party limo cost in Vernal?

$135–$200 per hour, 3-hour minimum. Standard Vernal nights run $540–$1,200; SLC weekends $800–$1,500. Gratuity and tax added at booking.

How many fit in the limo?

Up to 10. Larger groups can add a follow-vehicle — call us to coordinate.

Can we drink in the limo?

Yes, for parties of 21+ guests under Utah law. We provide chilled water and champagne flutes; the rest is BYO.

Multiple stops?

Standard. Send us the itinerary in pickup order and we'll route it. No upcharge for stops inside booked hours.

How early should we book?

Vernal-area bookings: 3–4 weeks ahead. SLC weekend trips, especially around UFC cards or playoff games: 6–8 weeks. Call (435) 219-9447.
