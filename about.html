About High Class

A locally owned luxury limousine service rooted in the Uintah Basin.

Our Story

Luxury arrived in dinosaur country.

High Class Limousine Services was founded on a simple idea: the people of Vernal and the Uintah Basin deserve the same luxury transportation you'd find in Las Vegas, Park City, or Salt Lake — without driving all the way there to get it.

Too many nights out, too many weddings, too many airport runs across Utah were being handled by basic sedans and rideshare apps. We wanted something more memorable. Something cinematic. Something local.

So we built it. A stretch limousine with gold mood lighting, a sound system that shakes the leather, and a chauffeur who actually cares about your night. Add the red rock sunsets, the dinosaur country backdrop, and a little personality — and you have something Vernal has never had before.

Why Vernal

Rooted in the Basin.

We live here. We grew up around these canyons, these red rocks, and these roads. When you book with us, you're not calling a national franchise — you're calling neighbors.

That also means we know every route. Whether you need an early run to Salt Lake City International, a scenic drive past Dinosaur National Monument, or a quick hop across town for prom photos at Steinaker, we've already made the trip.
