Uintah High Prom in a Limousine.

A pickup at home. A photo on the front lawn. A chauffeur who opens the door at the school.

High Class Limousine Services provides stretch limo transportation for Uintah High School prom. We pick up in Vernal, Maeser, Naples, and Jensen, run multi-stop pickup routes for friend groups, coordinate with dinner reservations at restaurants like Antica Forma and Betty's Cafe, and deliver students to the school venue. Stretch limousine pricing for prom typically runs $450–$900 for a 4-hour evening. Tuxedoed chauffeur, sparkling cider for under-21 riders, and a no-tolerance alcohol policy. Book 4–6 weeks ahead — prom weekend fills first.

Prom only happens twice. Once your junior year. Once your senior year. If you're going to do it, you might as well do it right — and around here, doing it right starts with not driving yourself.

We've been the limo at Uintah High prom for couples who wanted to roll up to the venue without worrying about parking, who wanted the kind of photo that holds up on a mantel for thirty years, and whose parents wanted the simple peace of knowing a licensed chauffeur was behind the wheel for the night. The car shows up. The chauffeur opens the door. The night begins.

How Prom Night Runs

From driveway to dance floor.

01

The Lineup

Tell us how many couples and where everyone lives. We map a pickup route through Vernal, Maeser, Naples, and Jensen so the last couple isn't waiting forty-five minutes in their living room.

02

The Pictures

We arrive twenty minutes early at the first house. That gives parents and grandparents time for the front-yard photo session before anyone climbs into the car. The limo itself is the backdrop most families want anyway.

03

Dinner

Reservations at Antica Forma, Betty's Cafe, or the Vernal Brewing Company are common stops. We hold curbside while you eat. No meter running, no rush — your booked time covers the wait.

04

The Venue

Drop-off at the school gymnasium or wherever prom is being held this year. Pickup at the end of the dance, with the same chauffeur, and we run the reverse route home.

Pricing

What a prom night runs.

Most Uintah High prom bookings come in around the four-hour mark — enough time for pickups, dinner, drop-off, and a return run after the dance. Pricing depends on the size of the limo and how late we're staying out.

  • 4-hour prom package (8-passenger stretch): $450–$650 typical
  • 4-hour prom package (10–14 passenger stretch): $650–$900 typical
  • Multi-stop pickup route: Included in your hourly rate
  • Sparkling cider, water, ice: Included free of charge
  • Gratuity: 18% standard, added at booking

Split between four to eight couples, that works out to surprisingly little per person — and includes a chauffeur, a clean car, and a guaranteed ride home.

What's Included

Tuxedoed chauffeur. Champagne flutes (used for sparkling cider only — strict no-alcohol policy for prom bookings). LED interior lighting. Sound system with a cable for your phone. Bottled water and ice. Clean exterior. The chauffeur in the same outfit at pickup as at drop-off, four hours later.

What We Don't Do

We don't transport alcohol. We don't allow alcohol in the vehicle for prom bookings — even if a parent says it's fine. The chauffeur has discretion to end the booking if alcohol appears. This isn't negotiable, and it's the same policy every reputable limo company runs.

Booking Window

When to reserve.

Uintah High prom typically lands in late April or early May. We start taking prom reservations in January, and the prime time slots — pickups between 5:00 and 6:30 PM on prom Saturday — are usually claimed by mid-March. If you're reading this in April and prom is in two weeks, call us anyway. Sometimes a slot opens up. But four to six weeks is the comfortable window.

Common Questions

Prom limo questions.

Can a parent ride along on the pickup route?

Yes, frequently. A parent in the front passenger seat is welcome and we encourage it for younger students. There's no extra charge.

What's the maximum group size?

Our largest stretch limo seats 14. For a group bigger than that, you'd want two limos or a different vehicle class — call us and we'll source it.

What if the dance ends earlier or later than expected?

Your booked window has flexibility built in. If the dance lets out 30 minutes early, we run early. If it goes long by 30 minutes, we wait. Significantly extending the booking after pickup will be billed at the hourly rate.

Do you serve other Basin schools?

Yes. We pick up for Union High School (Roosevelt), Altamont High, Duchesne High, and Tabiona prom as well. The same package structure applies — pricing reflects the longer drive time on those bookings.

Can I see the limo before I book?

Absolutely. Walk-throughs are by appointment. Call us and we'll meet you wherever the limo is parked that week.
