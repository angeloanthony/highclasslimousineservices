Oilfield Executive Transportation in the Uintah Basin

Confidential, on-time, locally driven. Built for the Basin's energy economy.

High Class Limousine Services provides confidential executive transportation for the Uintah Basin's energy industry. Salt Lake City International airport pickups, remote field site transfers, multi-day delegation shuttles, and standing corporate accounts with monthly billing. Hourly rates $135–$200/hr (3-hour minimum), flat rates for fixed routes. NDAs signed on request. Call (435) 219-9447.

The Uintah Basin runs on energy. So does its business calendar. Boards fly in from Houston and Denver. Investors come up from Park City. Field engineers drop in for site reviews. Out-of-state attorneys arrive ahead of closings. None of these people want to be picked up at SLC by a rideshare driver who has never heard of Vernal, can't find the Strawberry Reservoir exit, and white-knuckles every passing semi on US-40.

That's where we come in. High Class Limousine Services runs executive transportation purpose-built for the Basin's energy economy — confidential, on-time, and routed by someone who has already driven every mile that matters. Whether you're picking up a board member at SLC, shuttling a delegation between corporate housing and a remote field office, or staffing a multi-stop client visit, we handle the transport so your team handles the meeting. Call (435) 219-9447.

SLC Pickups

From the terminal to the Basin.

Salt Lake City International to Vernal is a three-hour drive on a clear day. We track the flight, meet the executive at baggage claim with their name on a sign, handle the luggage, and turn the next three hours into a quiet cabin where they can take a call, prep for the meeting, or simply rest. Coffee is hot, water is cold, WiFi is on.

For arrivals into Vernal Regional or SLC International, the same standard applies: we're at the curb when the wheels hit the ground.

Remote Site Transfers

To the field. And back.

Drilling sites, field offices, gathering facilities — anywhere your operation runs in Uintah or Duchesne County, we can get the executive there and back without burning a workday on logistics. We pre-confirm road conditions, gate access, and PPE requirements before the trip.

Multi-day delegations are routine: hotel pickups in the morning, on-site, lunch in town, second site in the afternoon, dinner reservation, evening drop-off. One vehicle, one driver, one number.

Confidential by Default

What gets said in the cabin stays in the cabin.

Our chauffeur is briefed on every executive booking. We sign NDAs on request. The partition stays up unless you say otherwise. The vehicle is fully tinted. We never name clients to other clients, never post pickups on social media, never volunteer who we drove yesterday.

For high-sensitivity bookings — M&A activity, regulatory inspections, investor due diligence — confidentiality is the default and the discount is zero.

Pricing

Standing accounts welcome.

One-off executive bookings run our standard hourly rate of $135–$200/hr with a 3-hour minimum, or flat rates for fixed routes (SLC International to Vernal: from $350 one-way; Vernal to remote field site round trip: quoted on the route).

For companies with recurring Basin transportation needs, we set up monthly billing accounts with priority booking, fixed rates, and consolidated invoicing. Most accounts pay back the setup conversation in the first month of saved logistics time.

Call (435) 219-9447 to discuss account terms or to quote a one-off run.

FAQ

Executive transport questions.

Do you handle Uintah Basin oilfield executive pickups?

Yes — it's one of our specialties. SLC International or Vernal Regional pickups, on to remote field offices, and back. We pre-confirm road conditions and access for every site visit.

Can we set up a corporate account?

Yes. Monthly billing, priority booking, consolidated invoicing. Call to discuss terms.

Will you sign NDAs?

Yes. Confidentiality is the default on executive bookings. Mutual NDAs available on request.

Remote field sites?

Routine. Uintah and Duchesne County drilling sites, gathering facilities, field offices — we'll confirm road conditions, gate access, and PPE requirements before the run.

How early should we book?

Standing accounts get same-day or 24-hour booking. New clients: 48–72 hours preferred for executive runs. Last-minute requests handled when the vehicle is available.
