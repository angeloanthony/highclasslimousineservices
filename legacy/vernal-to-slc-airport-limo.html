Vernal to SLC Airport Limousine Service

175 miles. Three hours. One flat rate. Door to terminal.

High Class Limousine Services runs door-to-terminal limousine transfers from Vernal, Utah to Salt Lake City International Airport (SLC). Flat-rate $350 one-way, $650–$800 round trip, real-time flight tracking, luggage handling, and a three-hour cabin built for actual rest or work. Available 7 days a week. Hourly rates ($135–$200/hr) also available for multi-stop trips. Call (435) 219-9447.

The drive from Vernal to Salt Lake City International is the most-traveled route out of the Uintah Basin and the one most worth not driving yourself. It's 175 miles. It's three hours minimum, four if Parley's Canyon is holding up traffic. It's the wrong way to start a trip and an even worse way to end one. The math says hire someone — and ours is the math we built our airport service around.

One flat rate, door-to-terminal. Real-time flight tracking. A black stretch limousine with leather, mood lighting, climate control, and enough quiet to actually sleep on the way home. Call (435) 219-9447 and the next time you fly, the only thing you handle is your bag.

The Route

US-40 west, then I-80 over the pass.

The standard route is US-40 west out of Vernal, through Strawberry Reservoir and over Daniels Summit, into Heber, down through Park City, and onto I-80 west into Salt Lake. About 175 miles, 3–3.5 hours. We know exactly which Strawberry construction zones cost what kind of time, when the chains-required flag flies on Daniels in winter, and which Parley's lane keeps moving when the rest stops.

For early-morning departures we leave Vernal four hours before takeoff. For peak-traffic afternoons (Friday returns from SLC into a Uintah weekend) we add a buffer hour. We never run it tight on a flight where missing the gate means another full day.

The Three Hours

Make the drive disappear.

Three hours of US-40 in your own car: white-knuckle, eyes-on-mirror, mind-on-the-meeting-you-haven't-prepped-for. Three hours in our cabin: laptop on, headphones in, two emails answered, a deck reviewed, a nap if you want one. Or if you're outbound for vacation: book in your lap, drink in your hand, the trip already started.

Coffee in the cabin if you ask. Phone charger ready. WiFi on. The driver is partitioned so you have actual quiet — talk if you want, work if you don't.

The Pickup Side

Land. Walk out. There we are.

We track the flight. If it's early, we move up. If it's late, we move back. You walk out of baggage claim, see the car at the curb, hand off the bag, get in. The cabin is already warm or already cool, whichever the season needs. Three hours later you're home.

For meet-and-greet inside the terminal, just request it on booking — we'll be at baggage claim with your name on a sign.

Pricing

Flat rate. No meter.

One-way Vernal to SLC International: from $350 flat rate. That includes flight tracking, luggage handling, and wait time on minor delays. No fuel surcharge, no per-mile add-on, no surprise tolls.

Round trip with airport wait: typically $650–$800 depending on the gap between drop-off and pickup. For multi-day stays where we return for a separate pickup, each leg is quoted individually.

Hourly billing is also available ($135–$200/hr) if your trip has multiple stops or doesn't fit a clean one-way structure. Tell us the itinerary and we'll quote whichever is cheaper for you.

FAQ

The drive, answered.

How long is the drive from Vernal to SLC airport?

About 175 miles, 3–3.5 hours under normal conditions via US-40 and I-80. Winter weather on Daniels Summit or summer construction in Parley's can add 30–60 minutes; we plan for it.

How much is a Vernal to SLC limo?

From $350 one-way (flat rate). Round trips with wait: $650–$800. Hourly available at $135–$200/hr.

Do you track flights?

Yes — every flight, real-time. Adjustments for delays are automatic and not billed.

What time should I be picked up for a morning flight?

Four hours before scheduled takeoff. That's drive time + check-in + security + buffer. For 6 a.m. flights, expect a 2 a.m. pickup. We're already awake.

Other airports?

Yes — Vernal Regional, Grand Junction Regional, Provo Municipal, and private/executive fields. Full airport service overview here.
