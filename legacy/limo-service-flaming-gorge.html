Flaming Gorge Limousine Tours

35 miles north of Vernal. Red rock canyon. The most photogenic drive in eastern Utah.

High Class Limousine Services runs scenic Flaming Gorge limousine tours from Vernal — 35 miles north on US-191 to the dam overlook, Red Canyon Visitor Center, and the Sheep Creek Geological Loop. Sunset tour (3hr) $400–$600; half-day (5hr) $675–$1,000; full-day (8hr) $1,000–$1,600. Fully paved scenic byway, limousine-accessible. Sunset tours are our most popular — book 3–4 weeks ahead in summer. Call (435) 219-9447.

Flaming Gorge sits 35 miles north of Vernal — across the Wyoming line, into one of the most photogenic corners of the American West. Red rock walls drop a thousand feet into a deep blue reservoir. The dam overlook delivers a view that ends careers in landscape photography. The Sheep Creek Loop winds through canyon, mountain, and meadow in 13 miles of constant cinema. Most people see it from a single overlook through a Honda Civic windshield. Our guests see it from a black stretch limousine, with cold drinks and gold mood lighting and time enough to actually take it in.

We run Flaming Gorge tours from Vernal year-round. Sunset tours are the most-booked. Half-day combinations with the dam, Red Canyon, and Sheep Creek are our specialty. Call (435) 219-9447 to plan the route.

Sunset Tours

Red rock at golden hour.

The most-booked Flaming Gorge package: depart Vernal late afternoon, drive US-191 north to the Greendale Junction and the dam overlook, then up to Red Canyon Visitor Center for the canyon view at sunset. Three hours total, two extended photo stops, and a return drive after dark with the cabin lit gold.

Summer sunsets at Flaming Gorge book up. Reserve 3–4 weeks ahead in peak season.

Half-Day & Full-Day

The Sheep Creek Loop.

For longer trips, we add the Sheep Creek Geological Loop — 13 miles of paved scenic byway through some of the most dramatic rock formations in the region — and time at the Manila overlook on the Wyoming side. Half-day (5 hours) covers all of it at a relaxed pace. Full-day adds Hideout Canyon Overlook and a stop for lunch at a Manila or Dutch John restaurant.

For wildlife: pronghorn, bighorn sheep, eagles, and the occasional moose are routine sightings on the Sheep Creek Loop. We don't promise them, but we'll slow down for them.

Pricing

Three packages, flat-rate.

Sunset Flaming Gorge Tour (3 hours): $400–$600. Vernal pickup, dam overlook, Red Canyon at sunset, return.

Half-Day Flaming Gorge Tour (5 hours): $675–$1,000. Adds Sheep Creek Geological Loop and Manila overlook.

Full-Day Flaming Gorge Tour (8 hours): $1,000–$1,600. Full Sheep Creek Loop, Hideout Canyon Overlook, lunch stop, and time at all major overlooks.

Hourly rate is also available ($135–$200/hr) if your day doesn't fit a clean package. Gratuity and tax added at booking.

FAQ

Flaming Gorge questions.

How far is Flaming Gorge from Vernal?

The southern end (Greendale Junction, dam overlook) is about 35 miles (45 minutes) north on US-191. Red Canyon Visitor Center: 50 miles. Manila: 70+ miles.

Is the limo OK on the scenic byway?

Yes — the main scenic byway (US-191, UT-44, Sheep Creek Loop) is fully paved and limousine-friendly.

How much does it cost?

Sunset (3hr): $400–$600. Half-day (5hr): $675–$1,000. Full-day (8hr): $1,000–$1,600.

When's the best time to go?

Late afternoon into sunset is the cinematic window. Spring–fall are the prime seasons; some upper roads (Sheep Creek Loop) close seasonally in winter.

How early should I book?

Summer sunset tours: 3–4 weeks. Off-season: 1–2 weeks usually fine.
