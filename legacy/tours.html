Scenic Utah Tours

Dinosaur country, red rock sunsets, and a chilled drink in your hand.

This is what makes us different. Vernal sits at the edge of some of the most cinematic landscape in America — Dinosaur National Monument, Flaming Gorge, red rock canyons, the Uinta Mountains. Most people see it all through a dusty windshield on a road trip. Our guests see it through tinted glass with gold mood lighting and a glass of something cold.

View
Dinosaur Country

Prehistoric Utah, modern luxury.

Dinosaur National Monument, the Quarry Exhibit, the petroglyphs — Vernal's most iconic attractions, seen from the back of a black stretch limousine. Stop when you want. Photos, snacks, the whole experience, without driving a single mile yourself.

  • Dinosaur National Monument
  • Utah Field House Museum
  • Red Fleet & Dinosaur Trackway
  • McConkie Ranch petroglyphs
View
Flaming Gorge

Where the red rock meets the water.

Flaming Gorge is one of Utah's most photogenic places — and yet most visitors only see it from a crowded overlook. We'll take you to the best viewpoints at the best times of day, with stops for photos and drinks at the top.

Sunset tours are our most-booked. Book early.

View
Sunset Cruises

Utah at golden hour.

No agenda. No itinerary. Just a black limousine winding through Vernal and the surrounding canyons while the sun paints everything gold. Pour a drink, roll down the partition, and let the Basin do what it does best.

  • 1, 2, or 3-hour sunset packages
  • Photography stops included
  • Perfect for anniversaries or proposals
  • Group bookings available
