How Much Does a Limo Cost?

Vernal, Utah pricing for stretch limousines, airport transfers, weddings, prom, and tours — broken down honestly.

Limousine service in Vernal, Utah typically costs $135–$200 per hour for a stretch limo with a 3-hour minimum. Airport transfers to Salt Lake City run $325–$500 one-way depending on pickup city. Weddings range from $500 for a 4-hour package to $1,500 for an all-day Basin-wide booking. Prom packages run $450–$900. Sunset tours of Flaming Gorge or Dinosaur National Monument are $200–$400 for short tours and $1,000–$1,600 for full-day excursions. Final pricing depends on hours, distance, vehicle size, and whether gratuity is included. This page walks through every service with realistic 2026 numbers.

The honest answer to "how much does a limo cost in Vernal" is: it depends. But "it depends" is a useless answer when you're trying to plan a wedding budget or figure out if a prom limo is realistic. So this page does the work for you — walks through every service we run, gives the actual price ranges, and explains what changes the number.

Every price below is what limousine service costs in Vernal and the broader Uintah Basin in 2026. These aren't aspirational big-city numbers; they reflect what limousine companies in this corner of Utah actually charge. The high end of each range is for premium dates (Saturdays in peak season) and longer routes. The low end is what a Tuesday-afternoon booking looks like.

    What Changes The Number

    Six things that move the price.

    The hourly rate is the base. From there, six factors push the final invoice up or down.

    • Day of the week: Saturdays are 10–20% higher than weekday bookings.
    • Season: Prom weekend and peak wedding season (May–September) are higher than off-season.
    • Vehicle size: A 14-passenger stretch is roughly 25% more per hour than an 8-passenger.
    • Total hours: Longer bookings get better per-hour rates. An 8-hour wedding day is cheaper per hour than a 3-hour minimum.
    • Distance: Long-haul transfers (SLC, Flaming Gorge, Dinosaur Monument) are flat-rate. In-town hourly stays at standard rates.
    • Gratuity: 18% is industry standard and built into our quotes. Some companies quote without it — always ask.

    What's Always Included

    Chauffeur (tuxedoed for formal events, business-attire for executive transfers), fuel, bottled water, ice, sound system with phone hookup, LED interior lighting, and a clean exterior. We don't tack on fuel surcharges or "vehicle prep" fees after the fact.

    What Costs Extra

    Champagne service ($45 per bottle), red carpet rollout (free for weddings, $25 for other events), additional stops beyond the original itinerary (billed at the hourly rate), and any toll roads (rare in our service area).

    Common Questions

    Pricing questions.

    Why is there a 3-hour minimum?

    A limousine has prep time on either side of every booking — wash, detail, position the vehicle, return it. A 1-hour booking still takes a half-day to staff. The 3-hour minimum reflects the actual cost of putting a clean limo on the road.

    Are there cheaper options for short trips?

    Yes. Our executive sedan service has a 2-hour minimum and runs $95–$130 per hour. For airport transfers under 30 miles, ride-share is usually cheaper if luxury isn't the goal.

    Do you offer flat rates instead of hourly?

    Yes, for airport transfers and known-distance tours. Vernal-to-SLC airport, Roosevelt-to-SLC, Duchesne-to-SLC, Flaming Gorge sunset tour, and Dinosaur Monument half-day are all flat-rate options.

    How does the deposit work?

    A 25% non-refundable deposit holds the date. Balance is due one week before the booking. We accept credit card, debit, and check.

    What's the cancellation policy?

    Cancellations more than 30 days out get the deposit refunded minus a $75 processing fee. Inside 30 days, the deposit is forfeit. Inside 7 days, the full booking amount is due. Weather-related cancellations are handled case-by-case.
